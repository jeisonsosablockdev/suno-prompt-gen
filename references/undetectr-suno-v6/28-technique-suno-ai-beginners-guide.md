## What Is Suno AI?

Suno is a web-based AI music generator. You describe what you want in text — genre, mood, instruments, vocal style — and Suno produces a complete song with vocals, instrumentation, and production. No musical instruments, recording equipment, or technical knowledge required.

The platform generates music using AI models trained on vast amounts of audio data. You provide creative direction through prompts; the AI handles composition, arrangement, production, and even vocal performance. The output is a finished audio file you can download, share, or distribute.

Suno is not the only AI music generator — **Udio**, **Stable Audio**, and others exist — but it is the most popular, with the largest user base and the most accessible interface for beginners. The latest [Suno V5 update](https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v5-review) brought dramatic improvements in audio quality and new features like Generative Stems.

## Getting Started: Your First Song in 5 Minutes

![Five steps to create your first Suno AI song in under five minutes](https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-ai-beginners-guide/first-song-5-steps.webp)

1. **Go to suno.com** and create an account. You can sign up with Google, Discord, or email. The free plan gives you enough credits to experiment.
2. **Click "Create"** on the main page. You will see a text input field.
3. **Type a description** of the music you want. For your first try, keep it simple: "upbeat pop song about summer adventures, female vocals, happy and energetic."
4. **Click Generate.** Suno will produce two variations of your song in about 30 to 60 seconds.
5. **Listen to both versions.** One will almost always sound better than the other. Click the download button on the one you prefer.

That is it. You just created your first AI-generated song. The entire process takes under 5 minutes from account creation to finished track.

## Simple Mode vs Custom Mode

![Suno Simple Mode versus Custom Mode side-by-side comparison](https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-ai-beginners-guide/simple-vs-custom-mode.webp)

Suno has two creation modes. Understanding the difference is essential.

### Simple Mode (Default)

You type a single text description and Suno handles everything: genre interpretation, lyrics, song structure, vocal style, instrumentation. It is the fastest way to generate music but gives you the least control. Good for casual experimentation and learning how Suno responds to different descriptions.

### Custom Mode (Recommended)

Custom Mode separates your input into two fields: **Style** (genre, mood, instrumentation, tempo) and **Lyrics** (the words the vocals sing, with structure tags). This gives you dramatically more control and produces significantly better results.

**Switch to Custom Mode as soon as possible.** The quality difference is enormous. Every track you intend to keep, share, or sell should be created in Custom Mode. See our [complete Custom Mode guide](./26-technique-custom-mode-guide.md) for the full breakdown.

## How to Improve Your Results

- **Be specific.** "Rock song" gives generic output. "1990s grunge rock, heavy distorted guitars, aggressive male vocals, 130 BPM, raw and angry" gives focused, high-quality output.
- **Use 4 to 7 descriptors.** Genre + tempo + mood + instruments + vocal style is the sweet spot. Too few = generic. Too many = confused.
- **Generate multiple variations.** Never settle for your first generation. Create 5 to 10 variations of each concept and keep the best one.
- **Learn from what works.** When you get a great result, analyze why. What was different about that prompt? Double down on patterns that produce quality output. Avoid the [most common Suno mistakes](./27-technique-10-suno-ai-mistakes.md) that waste credits and kill quality.
- **Switch to Custom Mode.** This is the single biggest quality upgrade. If you are still in Simple Mode, you are leaving most of Suno's potential on the table.

## Free vs Pro vs Premier: Which Plan Do You Need?

- **Free** — good for learning and experimenting. Limited daily credits. Non-commercial use only. Start here to see if you enjoy the process.
- **Pro ($8/month)** — 250 songs/month with commercial rights. Sufficient for casual creators and those just starting to monetize. Good stepping stone.
- **Premier ($24/month)** — 1,000 songs/month with commercial rights. Essential for serious creators building a catalog. At scale, you will generate many variations per concept and 250/month runs out fast.

**If you plan to sell or distribute your music, you must be on Pro or Premier.** Music generated on the free plan cannot be used commercially — this is a legal requirement, not a suggestion. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Suno AI free to use?

Suno offers a free plan with limited generations for personal, non-commercial use. To sell or distribute your music commercially, you need a Pro plan ($8/month for 250 songs) or Premier plan ($24/month for 1,000 songs). The free plan is great for learning and experimenting, but any music you intend to monetize must be generated on a paid plan.

### Do I need musical knowledge to use Suno?

No formal musical training is required. Suno generates complete songs from text descriptions. However, understanding basic music terms — genre names, tempo, mood descriptors — helps you write better prompts. You will also develop an ear for quality through practice, learning to distinguish between mediocre and professional-sounding output.

### How long does it take to generate a song on Suno?

Suno generates a complete track in under 60 seconds. The generation itself is fast — the time investment goes into writing good prompts, iterating on variations, and curating the best output. Plan to spend 5 to 10 minutes per keeper track when you include prompt refinement and quality selection.

### Can I use Suno music on YouTube and Spotify?

Yes, with a paid plan that grants commercial rights. For YouTube, you can upload directly. For Spotify and other streaming platforms, you need a distributor like DistroKid. Important: process your tracks through Undetectr before uploading to any platform to remove AI detection artifacts that can get your music flagged and removed.

### What is the difference between Simple Mode and Custom Mode?

Simple Mode takes a single text description and generates everything automatically — genre, lyrics, structure. Custom Mode lets you separately define the style/genre and write your own lyrics with structure tags. Custom Mode produces dramatically better results and is essential for any serious use. Switch to Custom Mode as soon as you are comfortable with the basics.

### How many songs can I make per month?

Free plan: approximately 5 to 10 songs per day with limited credits. Pro plan ($8/month): 250 songs per month. Premier plan ($24/month): 1,000 songs per month. Each generation attempt uses credits whether or not you keep the result, so your effective output depends on your hit rate — how many generations produce keepers.
