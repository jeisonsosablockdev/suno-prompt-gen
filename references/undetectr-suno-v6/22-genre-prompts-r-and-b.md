## The R&B Prompt Formula

![The R&B prompt formula: subgenre, BPM, groove, keys and bass, vocal runs, mood](https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-prompts-for-r-and-b/formula.webp)

Every prompt below is built from the same six slots. Fill them in and you get a focused, soulful track instead of a flat loop. Skip a slot and Suno fills it randomly — usually with the most generic option it knows.

#### The Formula

[Sub-style] + [BPM] + [Groove / Drums] + [Keys / Bass] + [Vocal Style / Runs] + [Mood]

- **Sub-style** — be precise: "contemporary R&B", "neo-soul", "trap-soul", "alternative R&B", "classic soul". This is the single most important slot.
- **BPM** — always a number. "72 BPM" reads cleaner than "slow". Trap-soul can be written as "140 BPM, half-time feel".
- **Groove / Drums** — "laid-back swung drums", "crisp programmed R&B drums", "live drums behind the beat", "half-time trap drums".
- **Keys / Bass** — "warm Rhodes chords", "lush synth pads", "jazzy extended chords", "round fingered bass", "melodic 808".
- **Vocal Style / Runs** — "silky female vocals with melismatic runs", "breathy male vocals", "layered three-part harmonies", or "no vocals" for an instrumental.
- **Mood** — "intimate and late-night", "warm and nostalgic", "moody and atmospheric", "heartbroken and raw".

**Worked example:**

Contemporary R&B, 72 BPM, laid-back swung programmed drums, lush synth pads and warm Rhodes, round sub bass, silky female vocals with melismatic runs and layered harmonies, intimate and late-night

That is the entire toolkit. Everything that follows is just this formula filled in for each sub-style, ready to paste into Suno's Custom Mode style field.

## R&B Specific Tips

![R&B prompt tips: ask for vocal runs and harmonies, swing the groove, warm production, use ad-libs](https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-prompts-for-r-and-b/tips.webp)

### Match the BPM to the sub-style

The fastest way to a generic R&B track is the wrong tempo. Contemporary R&B and soul ballads want the slow 60–90 lane, neo-soul holds a relaxed 70–95 pocket, trap-soul should be written at its fast count with a half-time feel (140 BPM that breathes at 70), and alt-R&B floats wide. Put the number in every prompt.

### Call your vocal runs and harmonies

Runs and harmonies are the soul of the genre, and Suno will not add them unless you ask. "Silky melismatic vocal runs", "layered three-part harmonies", "breathy ad-lib runs" — these phrases do the heavy lifting. In the lyrics field, drop a short **[Vocal Run]** or **[Ad-libs]** meta-tag where you want the riffing, and keep those lines syllable-light so the run has room to move.

### Make the groove swing

R&B is felt behind the beat. A straight, perfectly-quantized groove is the number-one giveaway of a flat AI render. Ask for "swung", "laid-back", or "behind-the-beat" drums, and for neo-soul and classic soul lean on "live drums" so the pocket loosens up. The looseness reads as human.

### Steer the production warmth

The line between neo-soul and contemporary R&B is mostly production vocabulary. Want warm and organic? Ask for "live drums", "Rhodes", "analog warmth", "vintage". Want glossy and modern? Ask for "clean programmed drums", "lush synth pads", "polished vocal stacks". Same chords, different world — and Suno follows the adjectives. If the track drifts toward a pop-R&B crossover, our [Suno pop prompts](./21-genre-prompts-pop.md) add the pre-chorus and hook structure pop leans on.

### Avoiding the AI tells

Two giveaways mark AI R&B: a vocal that sits dead-center on the grid with no breath, and harmonies that are *too* mathematically perfect. Counter the first by asking for "breathy", "intimate", "laid-back" vocals; counter the second by leaving space in the lyrics so the stacks feel sung rather than stamped. And generate 5–10 variations — vocal quality swings hard between takes, so audition several and keep the one with real feel. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What BPM should I use for R&B in Suno?

It depends on the sub-style. Contemporary R&B usually sits at 60 to 90 BPM, neo-soul lives in a relaxed 70 to 95 BPM pocket, and trap-soul leans on a half-time feel around 130 to 150 BPM that reads slow. Alternative R&B is wider — anywhere from 70 to 110 BPM depending on how atmospheric you want it — and classic soul tends to land between 60 and 100 BPM. Put the number in the prompt every time; Suno responds far better to '72 BPM' than to 'slow groove'.

### How do I get smooth vocal runs and harmonies in Suno?

Name them directly in the style field — 'silky melismatic vocal runs', 'layered three-part harmonies', 'breathy ad-lib runs' — and reinforce the structure with meta-tags in the lyrics field. Use [Hook], [Bridge], and a short [Vocal Run] or [Ad-libs] tag where you want the riffing to happen. Keep lyric lines conversational and leave space; runs sound natural over open, syllable-light phrasing and turn to mush when the bars are crammed.

### What is the difference between neo-soul and contemporary R&B prompts?

Neo-soul wants live-band warmth: ask for 'warm Rhodes chords', 'live drums with swing', 'jazzy extended chords', and an organic, slightly loose pocket. Contemporary R&B is more produced and modern — 'clean programmed drums', 'lush synth pads', 'polished vocal stacks', tighter timing. Same genre family, but the production vocabulary points Suno toward live and warm versus glossy and digital.

### Can Suno do trap-soul properly?

Yes, if you steer the two halves. Trap-soul is R&B singing over trap drums, so the prompt needs both: name the 'half-time trap drums', a 'warm melodic 808', and 'crisp triplet hi-hats' on the rhythm side, then 'breathy auto-tuned R&B vocals' and 'atmospheric pads' on the soul side. Leave either half vague and you get a generic beat with no mood, or a soft ballad with no knock.

### Why do my R&B vocals sound robotic or stiff in Suno?

Two usual causes. First, the groove is too rigid — ask for 'swung', 'laid-back', or 'behind-the-beat' feel so the pocket breathes the way real R&B does. Second, even a strong generation carries AI timing and spectral tells that read as subtly off.
