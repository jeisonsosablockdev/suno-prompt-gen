# Fichas de Producción Pro para los 13 Grandes Géneros en Suno V6

Estas 13 fichas técnicas condensan la fórmula completa de Style Box, Capa de Producción Pro, Exclusiones y Cues recomendados para cada género musical en Suno V6.

---

## 1. Pop (Summer Pop Anthem)
* **BPM / Key:** 120 BPM, G Major.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Upbeat dance-pop, 120 BPM, 4/4. Female lead vocal, bright and forward, playful conversational verse, powerful soaring belt on the hook. Driving programmed drums, four-on-the-floor kick, crisp clapping snare. Bouncing synth bassline locked to the beat. Bright acoustic guitar strums and sparkling synth arpeggios. Production: polished loud radio mix, vocal centered and loudest element, wide stereo guitars in chorus, punchy sub-bass, bright final chorus lift.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `distortion, screaming vocals, heavy metal guitars, dark ambient, lo-fi haze`

---

## 2. Rock (Seattle Grunge / 90s Alternative)
* **BPM / Key:** 116 BPM, Drop D Minor.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  90s Seattle grunge rock, 116 BPM, 4/4. Male lead, raspy and strained, quiet brooding verse, explosive distorted screaming chorus. Live acoustic drum kit in a big room, heavy snare on 2 and 4. Thick distorted electric rhythm guitars playing power chords; intro riff continues palm-muted under the verse vocal, never stops. Heavy fuzzed bass. Production: dynamic quiet-to-loud transitions, raw unpolished studio sound, loud live drums, hard stop ending.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `synths, auto-tune, electronic drum machines, pop pads, polished glossy mix`

---

## 3. Electronic / EDM (Deep Melodic House)
* **BPM / Key:** 124 BPM, F Minor.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Melodic deep house, 124 BPM, 4/4 club groove. Warm filtered female vocal chops. Punchy 909 kick, crisp swinging hi-hats, percussive shakers. Deep warm analog Moog sub-bassline. Lush Juno poly-synth chords opening filter cutoff progressively. Production: polished tactile club mix, tight mono low-end focus, wide stereo percussion, tasteful tape saturation, DJ-friendly extended mix.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `acoustic guitar, rock drums, heavy metal, country banjo, big room festival drops`

---

## 4. Hip-Hop (90s Golden Era Boom-Bap)
* **BPM / Key:** 90 BPM, D Minor.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  90s golden era boom bap, 90 BPM, 4/4. Male lead rap, rhythmic and conversational, confident flow, dry mono vocal chain in center. Dusty sampled vinyl drum break, cracking acoustic snare, unquantized swing. Warm upright acoustic bassline walking the root. Muted jazz trumpet and Rhodes piano stabs. Production: warm analog tape saturation, dusty record crackle, intimate dry booth acoustics, clean punchy kick.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `trap 808 slides, fast triplet hi-hats, auto-tune, electronic dance drops, synth leads`

---

## 5. Drill (UK Drill / Cold Night Drive)
* **BPM / Key:** 142 BPM, E Minor.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  UK drill, 142 BPM, 4/4. Aggressive male lead, close and menacing, rhythmic drill cadence. Heavy distorted 808 sub-bass with extreme semitone pitch slides. Sharp syncopated snare on the third beat, skipping hi-hat rolls with velocity variations. Cold minor piano melody with dark reverse reverb textures. Production: cold sparse mix, hard limiter impact, mono vocal centered and dry, heavy sub-bass impact.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `cheerful pop synths, acoustic folk guitar, brass horns, live rock drums, happy chords`

---

## 6. Lo-Fi (Study Beats & Chillhop)
* **BPM / Key:** 82 BPM, Eb Major.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Instrumental lo-fi chillhop, 82 BPM, 4/4. Dusty felt piano chords with soft pedal noise. Relaxed swinging boom bap drums, soft rimshot snare, lazy hi-hats behind the beat. Warm round electric bass. Gentle clean electric guitar with vintage chorus and tremolo. Production: vinyl crackle, subtle tape wow and flutter, low-pass filtered warmth, intimate bedroom studio atmosphere.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `vocals, singing, rap, bright pop synths, aggressive drums, sharp top-end, heavy metal`

---

## 7. Jazz (Acoustic Piano Trio)
* **BPM / Key:** 118 BPM Swing, Bb Major.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Instrumental jazz piano trio, 118 BPM swing, 4/4. Close-miked grand piano carrying melody and rich extended chords. Acoustic upright bass walking fluid walking-lines throughout. Live jazz drum kit played with wire brushes on coated snare, delicate ping on the ride cymbal. Rubato solo piano intro for 4 bars, trio enters bar five. Production: intimate acoustic club acoustics, warm natural room reverb, zero compression, clean dynamic ending.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `electric guitars, synthesizers, drum machines, vocals, brass section, distortion, heavy sub-bass`

---

## 8. R&B / Neo-Soul (Late Night Groove)
* **BPM / Key:** 88 BPM, Ab Minor.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Modern neo-soul, 88 BPM, 4/4. Female lead vocal, velvety and intimate, breathy verses, effortless melismatic runs and silky falsetto on the chorus. Warm Fender Rhodes electric piano chords with gentle phaser. Laid-back pocket drums with rimshot on 2 and 4. Deep round electric bass with subtle envelope filter. Production: close-miked vocal right in front, warm analog console warmth, wide stereo vocal harmonies on chorus.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `harsh distortion, aggressive synths, country twang, heavy rock drums, eurodance`

---

## 9. Country (Modern Radio Country)
* **BPM / Key:** 104 BPM, D Major.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Modern country rock, 104 BPM, 4/4. Male lead, weathered baritone with subtle southern drawl, storytelling verses, big soaring anthemic chorus. Live acoustic guitar rhythm strumming throughout. Weeping pedal steel guitar answering vocal phrases, never clashing with the voice. Driving live drums and bass holding the root notes. Production: clear forward vocal, punchy modern snare, wide stereo acoustic guitars, clean ending.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `synth pads, electronic beats, rap verse, 808 sub-bass, eurodance, heavy distortion`

---

## 10. Metal (Metalcore / Modern Heavy) — *Recomendado en `v6-wild`*
* **BPM / Key:** 140 BPM, Drop C.
* **Modelo Sugerido:** `v6-wild` (evita la compresión opaca de `v6` en guitarras densas).
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Modern metalcore, 140 BPM, 4/4. Male lead, harsh aggressive screaming verses, soaring clean anthemic vocal on the chorus. Drop C down-tuned heavy electric guitars playing fast rhythmic chugging riffs; verse keeps the rhythm riff under the scream. Lightning double-kick drum blasts with punchy tight snare. Heavy grinding bass. Production: wide wall of guitars panned hard left and right, aggressive quiet-to-heavy breakdown (v6-wild).
  ```
* **Exclude:** `acoustic pop, clean acoustic piano, cheerful brass, lo-fi crackle, disco beat`

---

## 11. Ambient (Cinematic Deep Space Drone)
* **BPM / Key:** 60 BPM, D Minor.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Instrumental cinematic ambient drone, 60 BPM, slow evolving. Deep warm analog synthesizer drone pad, sub-bass pulse below 50 Hz. Bowed cello textures with long shimmering delay tails. Distant tape-reversed piano fragments drifting across the stereo field. No rhythm, no drums, no percussion. Production: vast cathedral acoustic space, deep dimensional stereo imaging, smooth slow fade-out ending.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `drums, percussion, beat, rhythm, vocals, voice, singing, sharp transients, bright brass`

---

Screaming Hammond B3 organ with whirling Leslie speaker. Rich acoustic grand piano chords. Dynamic live drums building from gentle cymbals in the verse to full kit rolls in the chorus. Production: warm church acoustic reverberation, wide dynamic lift, joyous climax.

## 13. Classical / Cinematic (Neoclassical Piano & Cello)
* **BPM / Key:** 68 BPM, A Minor.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Instrumental neoclassical, 68 BPM, rubato 3/4. Solo upright felt piano played delicately with felt damping, audible wooden pedal mechanics and hammer texture. Answering solo cello with rich melancholic vibrato, entering on bar nine. No other instruments, no synthesizers, no percussion. Production: intimate scoring stage acoustics, natural room ambience, pure uncompressed dynamic range, quiet decay into silence.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `drums, percussion, bass guitar, synthesizers, vocals, singing, brass, electronic effects`

---

## 14. Dark Latin (Techno & Hard Club Fusion)
* **BPM / Key:** 128 BPM, G Minor (single-note bass).
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Dark latin, dark latin club, 128 BPM, 4/4. Late 20s LatAm male vocals, tight rapped delivery, percussive consonant attack, behind-the-beat swagger. Solid sub-bass sine punch locked to G, rolling tech bass with sharp sidechain, TR-808 straight hat grid, rim clicks, no hat triplets, aggressive mono synth stabs repeating triplets on G, dry mono lead with hard gating. Production: dark warehouse latin club atmosphere, dry punchy upfront mix, zero pads, loud kick.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `chord progressions, harmonic modulation, melodic chord stacks, acoustic percussion, hi-hat triplets, melodic singing vocals, acoustic guitar, tropical marimba`

---

## 15. Latin Clubbing (Global Bass & Dembow Club Tool)
* **BPM / Key:** 134 BPM, A Minor.
* **Style Box:**
  ```text
  Latin clubbing, latin club, 134 BPM, syncopated 4/4 dembow groove. Low-pitched vocal chops, short percussive phrases. Punchy 909 kick, Roland TR-727 congas and timbales, crisp open hi-hats, rolling 808 sub-bass pressure, gated stutters, stereo flicker ear-candy. Production: raw underground club mix, upfront transient punch, heavy sidechain pump, tape warmth, DJ loop-tool arrangement.
  ```
* **Exclude:** `commercial pop vocals, acoustic bachata, romantic ballad piano, country banjo, eurodance synths, happy pop hooks, cheesy horns`
