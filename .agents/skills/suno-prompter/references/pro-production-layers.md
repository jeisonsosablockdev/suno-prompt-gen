# La Capa de Producción Profesional en Suno V6 (6 Dimensiones)

Suno V6 interpreta el casillero de estilo de manera literal. Si no describes cómo debe sonar la grabación, el modelo adopta decisiones aleatorias. Una **Capa de Producción Pro** (*Pro Production Layer*) son 2 a 3 frases añadidas al final del estilo que definen la física y el espacio de la mezcla.

---

## 1. Las 6 Dimensiones de la Capa de Producción

| Dimensión | Decisión de Mezcla | Frases y Sintaxis que Funcionan en V6 |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Colocación Vocal** | Dónde vive la voz respecto a la banda | `female lead in front of the band, close-miked and dry, loudest element in the mix, brighter than instruments, instruments sit behind and below the voice` |
| **2. Low-End Ownership** | Quién es dueño de los graves (Bombo vs Bajo) | `punchy kick locked with sub bass; 808 carries the low end under a short click kick; woody upright bass carries the bottom; tight controlled low end with deep sub-bass` |
| **3. Espacio y Sala (Room)** | Tamaño y reverberación de la sala | `tight dry room; big live room drum acoustics; intimate studio booth; small smoky club; scoring stage acoustics` |
| **4. Ancho Estéreo (Width)** | Qué está al centro y qué en los laterales | `narrow verse opening up to wide glossy chorus; double-tracked rhythm guitars panned left and right; wide stereo percussion; pads wide, bass strictly mono` |
| **5. Textura y Era** | Color tímbrico y medio de grabación | `warm tape saturation; 1970s analog console warmth; clean modern digital sheen; dusty sampled drum texture; subtle tape flutter` |
| **6. Dinámica** | Contraste de volumen entre momentos | `verse sits lower and narrow, chorus opens up loud and wide; breakdown drops to near silence; each chorus hits bigger than the last` |

---

## 2. Palabras de Acción vs Palabras de Veredicto

El análisis de 98 temas destacados de Suno V6 demuestra que los "términos de veredicto" no tienen ningún efecto audible sobre el modelo:

| ❌ Términos Inútiles (Veredicto) | ✅ Términos Efectivos (Física y Colocación) |
|---|---|
| `professional`, `studio quality` | `in front of the band, close-miked and dry` |
| `radio-ready`, `viral hit` | `wide polished mix, bright final chorus lift` |
| `mastered`, `masterpiece` | `tight controlled low end, punchy modern drums` |
| `crisp and clear vocals` | `brighter than instruments, no reverb on verse` |
| `-14 LUFS`, `bass +12dB` | `kick and bass locked, short decay` |

## 3. Plantillas Universales de Capa de Producción por Familia

### A. Para Pop, Dance y Electrónica Comercial
```text
Production: polished loud club mix with wide glossy stereo, forward vocal with intimate presence, punchy kick and controlled sub-bass, crisp top-end sheen, narrow verse opening into a bright wide final chorus lift.
```

### B. Para Rock, Indie y Alternativo
```text
Production: live room studio acoustics, vocal centered and dry in front of the band, double-tracked guitars panned wide in the chorus, punchy uncompressed drums, dynamic quiet-to-heavy transitions, hard stop ending.
```

### C. Para Hip-Hop, Trap y Drill
```text
Production: tight controlled 808 sub-bass, crisp punchy drums, mono vocal chain centered and dry, wide stereo hi-hats, subtle tape saturation, loud dynamic impact on the hook.
```

### D. Para Acústico, Jazz, Country y Baladas
```text
Production: lived-in acoustic studio warmth, close-miked vocal with gentle analog presence, woody upright bass, quiet brushed drums, intimate dry verse widening into natural room reverb on the chorus.
```
